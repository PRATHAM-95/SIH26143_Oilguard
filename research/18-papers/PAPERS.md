# Papers & Scientific References

## Foundational Papers

### Oil Drift & Backtracking
1. **Dagestad et al. (2018)** — "OpenDrift v1.0: an open-source framework for oil spill trajectory modelling" — Geoscientific Model Development
   - Citation: 500+
   - Key finding: Built-in backward mode, validated against field data
   - Relevance: Our primary drift model

2. **Breivik et al. (2012)** — "Oil Spill Tracking and Source Identification Using a Dynamic Stochastic Model" — Ocean Dynamics
   - Citation: 300+
   - Key finding: BAKTRAK model for iterative source identification
   - Relevance: Backtracking methodology

3. **El Mohtar et al. (2021)** — "Bayesian Source Identification of Marine Oil Spill" — Marine Pollution Bulletin
   - Citation: 150+
   - Key finding: MCMC-based source estimation with uncertainty
   - Relevance: Uncertainty quantification

4. **Chen et al. (2025)** — "Review of Backtracking Methods for Oil Spill Source Identification" — Current Pollution Reports
   - Citation: New
   - Key finding: Comprehensive review of all backtracking methods
   - Relevance: Method comparison

5. **Ciappa & Costabile (2014)** — "Oil slick source identification using reverse trajectory risk analysis" — Environmental Modelling & Software
   - Citation: 200+
   - Key finding: Reverse trajectory for attribution
   - Relevance: Attribution methodology

### SAR Detection
6. **Wang et al. (2023)** — "Deep Learning-Based SAR Oil Spill Detection" — Remote Sensing of Environment
   - Citation: 100+
   - Key finding: DL-based detection pipeline
   - Relevance: Detection approach

7. **Trinder & Li (2022)** — "Oil Spill Detection Using SAR" — Journal of Marine Science and Engineering
   - Citation: 150+
   - Key finding: Comprehensive SAR oil detection review
   - Relevance: Sensor selection

### Oil Physics
8. **Johansen et al. (2015)** — "A stochastic droplet model for oil spills" — Marine Pollution Bulletin
   - Citation: 200+
   - Key finding: Wave entrainment droplet model
   - Relevance: Oil physics in OpenOil

9. **Li & Johansen (2017)** — "A stochastic model for wave entrainment of oil droplets" — Journal of Geophysical Research
   - Citation: 150+
   - Key finding: Wave entrainment formulation
   - Relevance: Oil weathering

10. **Rohrs et al. (2018)** — "Vertical mixing in ocean drift models" — Ocean Science
    - Citation: 100+
    - Key finding: Turbulent mixing parameterization
    - Relevance: OpenDrift configuration

### AIS Analysis
11. **Pallotta et al. (2013)** — "Maritime route learning from AIS data" — OCEANS
    - Citation: 300+
    - Key finding: Vessel trajectory mining from AIS
    - Relevance: AIS vessel correlation

12. **Nguyen et al. (2018)** — "Anomaly detection in maritime AIS data" — IEEE Access
    - Citation: 100+
    - Key finding: AIS anomaly detection methods
